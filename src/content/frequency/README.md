# English word frequency list

`en-5000.json` is the top 5000 English words, ordered most to least frequent, used by the difficulty scorer to band passages by vocabulary rarity.

Source: the first 5000 entries of [first20hours/google-10000-english](https://github.com/first20hours/google-10000-english), itself derived from [Peter Norvig's compilation](https://norvig.com/ngrams/) of Google's Trillion Word Corpus n-gram data, released for public and research use. A ranked word-frequency list is a factual compilation, not creative expression, so no separate licence applies.
